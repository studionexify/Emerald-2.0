Emerald — privacy policy

Emerald is a private business tool run by Studio Nexify for its own staff. Accounts are created by the owner; there is no public sign-up.

Data we keep: business records you enter (orders, bills, stock, notes) and photos or files you attach. Records are stored in our Supabase database; photos and files are stored in the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive: Emerald uses the drive.file permission, so it can only see and manage files it created itself, inside an "Emerald files" folder. It cannot read any other file in the Drive.

Sharing: data is never sold or shared with third parties and is used only to run the business.

Deletion: ask the owner to remove your account or data. Access to Google Drive can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
